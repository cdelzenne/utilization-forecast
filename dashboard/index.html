Definitions and method
Capacity
40 hours per week, less public holidays for the office and planned leave.
Confirmed
Hours booked on signed projects in the resourcing plan.
Weighted pipeline
Hours tentatively assigned to open opportunities, multiplied by the stage probability (Qualified 25%, Scoping 40%, Proposal 50%, Shortlisted 60%, Verbal 75%).
Forecast utilization
(Confirmed + weighted pipeline) ÷ capacity, summed over the people and weeks in view.
Target
Billable target by title (e.g. 85% Consultant, 65% Consulting Manager), weighted by each person's capacity.
Hours below target
Sum over people and weeks of how far forecast hours fall short of target hours; FTE equivalent assumes 40 h × 8 weeks.
Data
All names, clients and figures are synthetic, generated to prototype the workflow. In production this would read live from the resourcing tool, timesheets and CRM pipeline.
